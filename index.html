Quick grammar compass

Three jobs. Don't just memorize the words—notice what each one does.

BEFORE A NOUN

Possessive adjectives

my, your, his, her, its, our, their

“This is my towel.”
REPLACES A NOUN PHRASE

Possessive pronouns

mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs

“The blue towel is mine.”
RECEIVES THE ACTION

Object pronouns

me, you, him, her, it, us, them

“Please bring it to me.”
Remember: “his” can be used before a noun (“his bag”) or on its own (“the bag is his”). “Her” can be a possessive adjective (“her mat”) or an object pronoun (“I called her”). “Its” is not normally used as an independent possessive pronoun.
